# Impact audit

Every option simulated over a full 2026Q2–2030Q1 term against a passive
baseline, coalition Bhumjaithai + Pheu Thai + Others, seed 20260201.
Prerequisite flags are granted so gated options can be measured on their
own merits rather than showing as zero.

**Passive baseline** — headline 9186, legacy 2.21%, debt 73.8%, investment 18.4%, approval 54.4, reform stock 3.8.

## A note on units, because the card faces mislead

`reform effort +55` on the OECD card is not 55 points of reform stock.
Effort is a LEVEL. It is scaled by the coalition's reform capacity (0.55 to
0.75), then accumulates into the stock at about 6% a quarter against 1.5%
decay. Accelerating OECD accession at the start of a term with a 0.7-capacity
coalition therefore contributes about **+35 to the reform stock by 2030**, not
+55 — which is why a term that passed several large reforms finishes around
60 to 75 rather than in the hundreds. The Δreform stock column below is the
number that actually matters.
